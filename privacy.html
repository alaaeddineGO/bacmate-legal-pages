BacMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

BacMate is an educational application designed to help students prepare for the Algerian Baccalaureate examination. This Privacy Policy explains how BacMate collects, uses, stores, and protects user information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use BacMate, we may collect:

  • Account information, such as your email address and authentication information.
  • Profile information, such as your name, username, branch, study goals, and number of Baccalaureate attempts.
  • Study information, such as study plans, activities, progress, completed tests, notes, and educational preferences.
  • Premium and payment-related information necessary to process and verify Premium requests.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us or requesting support.

2. Google Sign-In

BacMate may allow you to create or access your account using Google Sign-In. When you use Google Sign-In, we receive the account information provided by Google that is necessary to authenticate your account, such as your email address and basic profile information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your BacMate account.
  • Provide educational content and features.
  • Personalize your study experience according to your branch and preferences.
  • Save and display your study plans and progress.
  • Track completed tests and educational activities.
  • Provide Premium educational content and manage Premium requests.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • Maintain and improve the security and functionality of the application.

4. Data Storage and Service Providers

BacMate uses third-party services to provide authentication, database, storage, and application functionality. These services may process information on our behalf according to their applicable privacy policies and security practices.

BacMate uses Supabase for backend services, including authentication, database storage, and related application services.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary for operating BacMate, such as authentication, database, storage, and hosting providers.

6. Premium and Payment Information

BacMate may provide Premium educational content through payment methods offered by the application. Payment verification information may be used to process and verify Premium requests.

BacMate does not request or store banking passwords, card PINs, or similar sensitive authentication credentials.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Data transmitted between the application and supported services is protected using secure communication protocols such as HTTPS/TLS.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your BacMate account and associated personal data. Account deletion may remove your profile and data associated with your account, subject to any information that must be retained for legal, security, or legitimate operational purposes.

For account deletion requests, please contact BacMate support using the contact information provided in the application or on the official account deletion page.

9. Children's Privacy

BacMate is an educational application intended for students preparing for the Baccalaureate examination. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how BacMate handles your information, please contact BacMate support through the contact options available in the application.
