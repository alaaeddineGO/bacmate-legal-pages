BacMate - Account Deletion

This page explains how BacMate users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your BacMate account, contact BacMate support through the contact options available inside the BacMate application. Please provide the email address associated with your BacMate account so that we can identify your account and process your request.

What Data Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, we will delete personal data associated with your account, including:

  • Account and profile information.
  • Name and username.
  • Study plans and study activities.
  • Study progress and completed tests.
  • Notes and other user-created content.
  • Premium account status and associated account information.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained when required by law, necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate business purposes. Any retained information will be kept only for the period necessary for the applicable purpose.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period after the request is received and verified.

Contact

For account deletion requests or questions about your data, please contact BacMate support through the contact options available in the BacMate application.
